# Response to Area Chair

We thank the area chair for the thorough meta-review and for synthesizing the three reviewers' feedback. We address each reviewer's individual points in our responses to Reviewers hzjD, 8ABX, and aVAU. One point raised directly by the meta-review does not map to any single reviewer's comments, so we address it here.

### Croissant `hasSyntheticData` flag

We thank the area chair for raising this, but we believe it rests on a terminology ambiguity we should clarify rather than a flag we should set. "Synthetic" in "synthetic peptides" means these peptides were synthesized in the lab rather than extracted from nature; it does not mean the data itself was algorithmically generated or simulated in a computer, which is what the Croissant `hasSyntheticData` flag is intended to capture. Every retention-time label in our dataset is a genuine experimental measurement from reverse-phase liquid chromatography on these lab-synthesized peptides; nothing in the dataset, neither the molecules nor the labels, is computationally generated or simulated. Setting `hasSyntheticData` would therefore misrepresent the dataset as algorithmically produced when it is in fact entirely experimental. We will not set this flag, but we agree the "synthetic" terminology invites exactly this confusion, and will add a clarifying sentence to the dataset documentation and paper distinguishing "chemically synthesized" (true of our peptides) from "synthetically generated data" (not true of any part of our dataset). If the area chair intended `hasSyntheticData` to capture something different from our reading here, we would welcome the correction and will set the flag accordingly.
